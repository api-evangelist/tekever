Free-entry exhibition at the Caldas da Rainha Cultural and Congress Centre until 30 August. The work of 16 Ukrainian photographers is accompanied by original texts from five Portuguese writers. The President of the Portuguese Republic, António José Seguro, visited the exhibition and highlighted the role of photography in documenting war and preserving memory.
